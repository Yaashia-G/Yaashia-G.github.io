### [Franka Controller Playground]({{ '/teaching/franka-pd-playground/' | relative_url }})

An in-browser simulation of the Franka FR3 for comparing controllers side by side: joint PD, impedance, hybrid force control, and the CERG safety filter. Tune the gains, move the target pose, push or kick the end effector, and watch the step response, contact forces and energy live. Built for a guest lecture on robot control.
